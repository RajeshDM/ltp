## What is running

58 one-seed training runs (`configs/p2_*.yaml`, generated by
`tools/make_protocol_configs.py`) + 58 evaluations, as jobs in
`campaign/manifest.yaml` (`protocol_jobs()` in `campaign/make_manifest.py`).
Pull-based SLURM workers (`campaign/submit.sh <preset> <n>`) claim them; each
worker queues its own successor, so nothing needs resubmitting. Goal: every
first-seed result by 2026-12-03 (see ICLR27/PAPER_PLAN.html, "Plan").

Per run: train (`train_queue.sh`) → `campaign/jobs/protocol_eval.sh`
(sweep the 10 snapshots on the training domains' `@val_ood`, select with
`tools/select_checkpoint.py`, test that epoch on `@easy/@medium/@hard`) →
`cache/results/<cfg>_final/`.

Gates: `gate_p2_smoke` (done) and `gate_p2_collect`, released when
`campaign/reports/p2_collect.txt` appears. That file is written by
`campaign/jobs/p2_collect.sh` (SLURM job 22165257 at handoff, on an el9 share
node), which plans Spanner's Val-OOD (~2 h of LAMA) and the extended / typed
training sets. 42 of the 58 runs (everything training on Spanner, and the
typed twins) wait for it. If it fails, resubmit:

```
sbatch -A eecs -p share -c 12 --mem=96G -t 0-06:00:00 -J p2_collect \
  --constraint=el9 --exclude=cn-e41 -o logs/protocol/collect_%j.out campaign/jobs/p2_collect.sh
```

## Hazards learned today

- Fast Downward runs only on el9 nodes (CLUSTER.md): `--constraint=el9` for
  anything that may plan.
- `eecs` GPUs are RTX 2080 / 11 GB: Rovers and multi-domain runs OOM; preset
  removed.
- Snapshot interval must be a multiple of the 10-epoch save cadence.
- Rates in result dumps are fractions (1.0 = 100%); the dashboard multiplies.
